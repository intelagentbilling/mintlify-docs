OHIP doesn't cover everyone. Refugees, serving military members, RCMP officers,
veterans and injured workers are all paid for by someone else — and if you work in
an ED, a walk-in, or any acute care setting, you will see them. Register before
you do, because both programs have submission windows that run from the date of
service, not from the date you noticed.

## Medavie Blue Cross

Medavie Blue Cross administers four federal health programs:

| Program | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| **IFHP** — Interim Federal Health Program | Refugees and protected persons |
| **CAF** — Canadian Armed Forces | Active and reserve military members |
| **RCMP** | Royal Canadian Mounted Police members |
| **VAC** — Veterans Affairs Canada | Veterans |

### Register

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the provider registration form">
    Go to [medaviebc.ca/en/health-professionals/register](https://www.medaviebc.ca/en/health-professionals/register).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say you're new">
    Select **I don't have a provider ID and I'd like to register**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the form">
    Your credentials, practice address, and CPSO number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept and submit">
    Read the Terms and Conditions, then submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once approved you get a Medavie Blue Cross provider number and ePay access.
Prefer the phone? **1-888-614-1880**, Monday to Friday, 6:00 am to 9:00 pm
Eastern.

For IFHP specifics, IRCC publishes a
[health care professionals page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees/help-within-canada/health-care/professionals.html).

<Warning>
**Six months from the date of service** to submit — longer for some program
subtypes, but plan around six. Build these claims into the same daily habit as
your OHIP claims rather than letting them pile up in a separate stack.
</Warning>

<Note>
**IFHP coverage lags.** After a certificate is issued it takes at least two
business days to activate in the Medavie system, and the beneficiary is eligible
throughout that window. If a refugee patient's coverage isn't showing in the
portal yet, don't turn them away — see the *IFHP Information Handbook for Health
Care Professionals* for how to handle the claim.
</Note>

## WSIB

The Workplace Safety and Insurance Board pays for care related to workplace
injuries and illnesses. ED, walk-in, occupational medicine and most acute care
physicians see WSIB patients regularly. Registration runs through **TELUS Health**,
which operates WSIB eServices for the WSIB.

### Register

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start at the WSIB physicians hub">
    [wsib.ca/en/physicians](https://www.wsib.ca/en/physicians), then follow the
    link to register for electronic billing through TELUS Health.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the TELUS Health registration">
    CPSO number, practice address, banking details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    You're assigned a WSIB provider number and set up for electronic submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

By phone: TELUS Health Solutions, **1-866-240-7492**.

### What to expect

- WSIB has its own forms. **Form 8**, the initial report, is the one you'll
  complete most often.
- Electronic submission pays a higher fee and pays faster than paper. There's no
  reason to submit on paper.

### Billing WSIB through IntelAGENT

WSIB claims go out through the same submission run as your OHIP claims — the only
difference is the **Payment program** field on the claim. Choose **Workplace
Safety and Insurance Board (WCB)** instead of Ontario (HCP), and the form adjusts
to what WSIB needs.

<Card title="Submit a Claim" icon="file-pen" href="/docs/guides/bill-one-visit">
  Where the Payment program dropdown lives, and what changes when you switch it.
</Card>

<Note>
Medavie Blue Cross programs — IFHP, CAF, RCMP and VAC — are billed through
Medavie's own ePay system, not through MC EDT, so those claims are submitted
outside IntelAGENT.
</Note>
