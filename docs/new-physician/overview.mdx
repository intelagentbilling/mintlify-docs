Nothing you bill gets paid until you're registered with the payer, and the
registrations depend on each other — the ministry won't issue a billing number
without a CPSO certificate, and you can't enrol in MC EDT without the identifiers
that arrive with the billing number. Work through them in order.

This section covers the setup that happens **outside** IntelAGENT. Once you have
a billing number and EDT access, [OHIP Onboarding](/docs/getting-started/onboarding)
takes about ten minutes and you're billing.

<Info>
Written for physicians starting independent practice in Ontario, in any
specialty. It assumes you hold a CPSO Certificate of Registration or are about to.
</Info>

## The order, and how long each takes

| # | Step | What it is | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **CPSO** | Certificate of Registration for independent practice | Apply 3–4 months before training ends |
| 2 | **OHIP billing number** | Ministry application with CPSO, banking and address | 2–6 weeks |
| 3 | **GO Secure** | The ministry sign-in account | Same day |
| 4 | **MC EDT enrolment** | Electronic claim submission, using identifiers from step 2 | Same day |
| 5 | **IntelAGENT** | Subscribe, designate, activate | Same day |
| 6 | **Medavie Blue Cross** | IFHP, CAF, RCMP, VAC | 1–3 weeks |
| 7 | **WSIB** | Registration through TELUS Health | 1–2 weeks |
| 8 | **RAMQ** *(if needed)* | Only if you see Quebec residents | 4–8 weeks, or per-claim by mail |

Steps 1 through 4 are sequential — each one needs something the previous one
produces. Steps 6 through 8 are independent; start them whenever, and start them
early if you're heading into an ED, a walk-in, or anywhere near a campus.

<Tip>
Processing times stretch in June, July and August, when every graduating cohort
applies at once. If you're finishing training, start step 1 in the winter.
</Tip>

## Where IntelAGENT fits

IntelAGENT is the submission side, not the registration side. You still get your
own billing number and your own MC EDT enrolment — they're yours, in your name.
What IntelAGENT does is take the encounter you enter, build the ministry-format
claim file, submit it on schedule, pull the remittance advice back, and reconcile
what was paid against what you claimed.

Under PHIPA you remain the Health Information Custodian; IntelAGENT acts as your
agent, which is what the [MC EDT designation](/docs/getting-started/designation)
authorizes.
