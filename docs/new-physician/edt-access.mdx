OHIP accepts electronic claims only. They travel through **MC EDT** (Medical
Claims Electronic Data Transfer), and MC EDT sits behind a **GO Secure** account
— the ministry's sign-in service, now branded OPS BPS Secure. Both are one-time
registrations.

<Info>
**Prerequisite:** the ministry letter from your
[billing number application](/docs/new-physician/ohip-billing-number). Step 2
below can't be completed without the unique identifiers printed on it.
</Info>

## Register for GO Secure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the registration">
    Go to [ebse.health.gov.on.ca](https://www.ebse.health.gov.on.ca) and click
    **New User Register**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in your information and accept the Terms of Service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Click the verification link in the email the ministry sends you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Use the email address and password you just set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the PIN">
    A second email carries an 8-digit one-time PIN. Enter it and click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You should see **Success! Email Verification complete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Same-day, assuming both emails arrive promptly.

## Enrol in MC EDT

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to GO Secure">
    Use the account you just created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose New User">
    Select **New User** from the drop-down menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your unique identifiers">
    These are on the ministry letter that came with your billing number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the policy">
    Review and accept the Acceptable Use Policy for Electronic Business Services.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You should see **Successful Enrolment**. Log out and close the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it stuck">
    Reopen your browser, sign in again, and confirm you land on the
    **Administration and MOHLTC Services** screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The full ministry reference is the
[MC EDT reference manual](https://www.ontario.ca/page/medical-claims-electronic-data-transfer-mcedt).

From that screen you can submit claim files, run remittance advice inquiries,
respond to requests for additional information, and authorize designees — which
is the next step.

## Let IntelAGENT submit for you

MC EDT gives you a channel; it doesn't give you a claim file. Submitting on your
own means producing ministry-format files yourself and uploading them by hand,
every cycle.

Instead, authorize IntelAGENT as your **designee** in GO Secure. You keep your own
MC EDT enrolment and your own login — the designation simply lets IntelAGENT
upload claim files and download remittance advice on your behalf. Under PHIPA you
remain the Health Information Custodian and IntelAGENT acts as your agent.

<Card title="MC EDT Designation" icon="key" href="/docs/getting-started/designation">
  The seven-step walkthrough: add **agent@intelagent.ca** as a designee, confirm
  the designation, then grant MC EDT permissions when the acceptance email
  arrives.
</Card>

The [onboarding wizard](/docs/getting-started/onboarding) checks with the ministry
that the designation is live and activates your account when it is.

## Know the 18th

The ministry processes claims monthly. **Anything received after 5:00 pm on the
18th may not be approved until the next cycle.**

A claim submitted November 18 appears on the December remittance advice. The same
claim submitted November 19 may not appear until January — a full month of cash
flow, lost to one day.

<Tip>
This is the practical argument for billing daily rather than in batches. If you
enter claims as you go, you're never near the cutoff. See
[Your first year](/docs/new-physician/first-year).
</Tip>
