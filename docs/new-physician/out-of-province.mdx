Most out-of-province patients are easy: you bill OHIP, and OHIP recovers from the
other province. Two exceptions aren't — Quebec residents, and international
students on UHIP. Both are common enough in an ED or a walk-in that it's worth
knowing the route before one is in front of you.

## Other provinces: reciprocal medical billing

Every province and territory except Quebec participates in the reciprocal medical
billing agreement. You submit the claim as usual and set the **Payment program**
to the patient's home plan — Alberta (AHCIP), British Columbia (MSP), Manitoba
(MHSIP), and so on down the list. OHIP handles the recovery.

<Note>
The payment program list also includes an **Outaouais Region (PQ)** entry. Quebec
as a whole is not part of reciprocal medical billing for physician services —
confirm with the ministry before using it, and otherwise use one of the three
routes below.
</Note>

## Quebec residents

Quebec is outside the agreement, so **OHIP will not recover from RAMQ on your
behalf**. If a Quebec resident presents to your ED, walk-in or clinic, you have
three options.

### Option 1 — Bill the patient directly

Collect at the point of care. The patient then claims reimbursement from RAMQ
themselves, using [RAMQ's reimbursement page for
citizens](https://www.ramq.gouv.qc.ca/en/citizens/health-insurance/reimbursement-covered-services).

RAMQ reimburses up to Quebec rates regardless of what you charged, so the patient
absorbs any difference. Simplest for you, worst for them.

### Option 2 — Register with RAMQ as an out-of-province provider

Register directly with RAMQ as a health professional practising outside Quebec.
RAMQ then pays you directly at Quebec rates and you collect nothing from the
patient. Forms can be completed in French or English; registration takes **4 to 8
weeks**.

Worth it if Quebec residents are a regular part of your practice — border
hospitals, Ottawa, anywhere with a commuting population.

### Option 3 — Bill RAMQ per claim with Form 4292

The middle path, for physicians who see Quebec patients occasionally. No
registration, no collecting from the patient: complete
[Form 4292](https://www.ramq.gouv.qc.ca/sitecollectiondocuments/professionnels/formulaires/4292.pdf)
— the English version of Form 2688, *Out-of-Province Claim for Physician Services*
— mail it in, and RAMQ pays you at Quebec rates.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Length** | One page, completed in full by you |
| **Patient signature** | Not required |
| **Codes** | Either the OHIP fee code you would have used, or the equivalent RAMQ act code |
| **Submission** | Paper mail only — no email, fax or upload |
| **Deadline** | One year from the date of service |
| **Payment** | Typically 4 to 12 weeks |

<Tip>
Keep a copy of everything you mail. It's a paper process with no tracking, and a
copy is the only evidence you sent it.
</Tip>

RAMQ contact: **1-800-561-9749**.

## UHIP: international university students

The **University Health Insurance Plan** covers international students, visiting
faculty and their dependants at Ontario universities. It is not OHIP — it's
administered privately by Cowan Insurance Group and pays physician services at up
to **125% of the OHIP rate**.

If you work near a campus, in a walk-in clinic, or in an ED, you will see UHIP
members.

### Three ways to be paid

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Preferred Provider Network">
    Apply to Cowan at **uhip.ramu@cowangroup.ca** or **1-833-377-8447**. Once
    enrolled you bill Cowan directly and your clinic is listed in the UHIP
    directory. Best if you see UHIP members regularly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Direct-bill per encounter">
    For occasional patients, submit the claim through the Cowan provider portal
    without joining the network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Have the patient pay and claim">
    The member pays you at the time of service and submits to Cowan themselves.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cowan publishes a page
[for medical professionals and billing agencies](https://uhip.ca/for-medical-professionals-and-billing-agencies-only/)
with current requirements.

<Note>
UHIP and RAMQ claims are billed to those payers directly, not through MC EDT, so
they're submitted outside IntelAGENT. Track them somewhere you'll actually look —
an unmailed Form 4292 is invisible until the deadline passes.
</Note>
