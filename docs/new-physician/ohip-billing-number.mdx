title: "Get your OHIP billing number"
description: "The three things you need in hand first, the ministry application, and what to check on the letter that comes back"
---

Your OHIP billing number is the identifier every claim is paid against. The
ministry issues it once you can show a CPSO certificate, an Ontario practice
address, and a bank account to deposit into.

## Before you apply

Start these three in parallel — the CPSO certificate is the long pole.

### CPSO Certificate of Registration

Apply through the [College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario](https://www.cpso.on.ca/Physicians/Registration/Apply-for-Registration).

- Finishing postgraduate training in Ontario: apply for the independent practice
  certificate **three to four months** before training ends.
- Coming from another province: allow **four to five months** for the CPSO
  Registration Committee review.

### An Ontario practice address

Your primary work location — hospital, clinic, or group practice site. A home
address on its own isn't accepted. You can change it later if your main site
changes.

### Banking information

OHIP pays by direct deposit only. Have a void cheque or a bank letter confirming
the account.

<Warning>
Your billing number carries a **specialty code**, assigned from your CPSO
registration, and that code decides which fee codes you're allowed to bill. Check
it on the ministry letter the moment it arrives — a wrong specialty code rejects
claims quietly and repeatedly until it's fixed.
</Warning>

## Apply

Complete the [Application for OHIP Billing Number](https://forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca/en/dataset/on00574)
online. The ministry's reference page is
[ontario.ca/page/ohip-billing-number-registration](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ohip-billing-number-registration).

Submit with it:

- The completed application form
- A copy of your CPSO Certificate of Registration
- Banking information — void cheque or bank letter
- Proof of your practice address
- Government-issued photo identification

**Processing takes 2 to 6 weeks**, longer through the summer onboarding peak.

Questions go to the Service Support Contact Centre at **1-800-262-6524**, 8:00 am
to 5:00 pm Monday to Friday.

## What comes back

A letter from the ministry with your **OHIP billing number** and the **unique
identifiers** you'll need to enrol in MC EDT.

<Check>
Keep this letter. You need it twice — once for
[MC EDT enrolment](/docs/new-physician/edt-access), and again any time the
ministry asks you to confirm your identifiers.
</Check>

## If you're joining a group

Family Health Organizations, hospitalist groups and ED groups have a billing
number of their own. The group lead registers and maintains it; you're added as a
member.

Depending on the group contract you may bill to your personal number, the group
number, or both — and which services go where is not always obvious. **Confirm it
before your first shift.** In IntelAGENT the group number lives on your profile as
the **MOH Group**; see [Profile Settings](/docs/account/profile-settings).

<Tip>
Billing the wrong number is one of the more expensive early mistakes, because the
claims are usually accepted — they just pay the wrong entity, and unwinding it
means a bulk adjustment months later.
</Tip>
