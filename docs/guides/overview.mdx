## Where to start

If you're new, the shortest path to a submitted claim is:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish onboarding">
    Your account has to be activated before anything can be submitted. See
    [Onboarding](/docs/getting-started/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bill one visit by hand">
    Use [the full claim form](/docs/guides/bill-one-visit) once, so you've seen
    what a claim is made of.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Then stop doing it by hand">
    Move to [spreadsheet import](/docs/guides/bulk-upload) or
    [rosters](/docs/guides/create-a-roster), depending on whether your day comes
    out of an EMR or off a floor.
  </Step>
</Steps>
